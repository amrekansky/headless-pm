## Core Questions (20-25 min)

### Their current workflow
1. Walk me through how you currently handle [domain].
2. What does that process look like from start to finish?
3. Where does it break down or slow you down?

### Past behavior (signals real pain)
4. Tell me about the last time [pain moment happened].
5. What did you do when that happened?
6. How much time did that cost you?
7. What have you tried to fix it?

### Stakes and priority
8. How often does this come up?
9. What happens if it doesn't get solved?
10. Is this something your team talks about?

## Dig Deeper (if time)
- "Tell me more about that..."
- "Why did you choose that approach?"
- "What would have to be true for you to stop doing that workaround?"
